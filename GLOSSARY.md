# Bilingle

Bilingle pairs strangers for live language-exchange conversation: each person teaches a language they know and practises one they are learning.

## Language

**Language Pair**:
One fluent-to-learning combination a user is willing to practise, such as English to Turkish. A user may offer several at once.
_Avoid_: Pair (on its own), combination, language setting

**Queue**:
The set of users currently waiting to be matched, each with the Language Pairs they offered.
_Avoid_: Pool, lobby, waiting list

**Match**:
Two users joined on one Language Pair that mirrors the other's, sharing one conversation (video, chat, subtitles). A Match lasts until either side Skips or leaves.
_Avoid_: Pairing, session, room, call

**Partner**:
The other user in your Match. Only ever exists while you are in a Match.
_Avoid_: Stranger, peer, other user

**Skip**:
Ending your Match by choice. You return to the Queue; your Partner is left idle and must rejoin the Queue themselves.
_Avoid_: Next, stop, leave, disconnect
